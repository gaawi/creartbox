Season September 10, 2026

CreArtBox announces its twelfth season

Four productions at The DiMenna Center, eleven works from the open call, one world premiere, touring dates in Illinois, Kentucky and the Hudson Valley, a residency in Finland, and the seventh Festival ADAR in Asturias.

The twelfth season runs from October 2026 to August 2027 and takes in ten dates.

At the centre of it is the New York Series: four productions at The DiMenna Center for Classical Music, each at 7:30 pm.

The four nights in New York

Currents (30 October 2026) is built around one idea, current, moving air, moving water, energy going from one place to another, and pairs Eric Moe's Laminar Flow in Upsidedown Creek and a new piece by the ensemble's flutist Guillermo Laporta with Brahms and both Schumanns.

Winterlight (11 December 2026) opens with the world premiere of Hannah Selin's Tectonic Lullaby for piano quintet and closes with Stravinsky's Petroushka in a chamber arrangement.

Pressure and Release (23 April 2027) is given over entirely to the open call: seven works, seven composers, one evening.

Feverdream (7 May 2027) closes the season with Sibelius's G minor Piano Quintet.

Away from New York

The ensemble opens the Engelbach-Hart Music Festival on Illinois College's Fine Arts Series (6 November 2026, free admission), plays the Chamber Music Society of Louisville (28 February 2027), and brings Masked Sounds, a flute, cello and piano programme, to Saugerties in the Hudson Valley (18 April 2027).

In Finland, Laporta and pianist Josefina Urraca spend five days at the Kuopio Conservatory (5 to 9 October 2026) giving masterclasses and a workshop on building a chamber piece on stage. They play one public concert during the residency, in the chamber hall of the Kuopio Music Centre next door.

In Queens, CreArtBox works with Culture Pass and other local organisations to make seats free or affordable for low-income communities.

The season ends in Spain. The seventh Festival ADAR runs from 2 to 15 August 2027 across rural Asturias: residencies, micro-concerts and "ADAR en Ruta" stops in small towns, played in churches, hórreos and mountain villages rather than concert halls.

Tickets

Tickets for Currents are on sale now through Eventbrite. Dates for the remaining New York Series productions go on sale in turn; touring dates are ticketed by their presenters.
