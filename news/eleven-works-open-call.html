Open call September 15, 2026

Eleven works this season came through the open call

More than in any season before, and in April an entire evening is given over to them.

CreArtBox reads every score sent to its open call. Eleven of the works played this season arrived that way, more than in any season before, and they are not kept apart in a new-music evening: they sit next to Brahms, Schumann, Stravinsky and Sibelius, on the same programmes, in the same halls.

The eleven are by Eric Moe; Oliver Caplan; Yurui (Rain), Alex Burtzos, Luke Carlson, Gilad Cohen, Samantha Leigh Sack, Paul Novak and Sam Wu; and Zygmund de Somogyi and Eli Tausen á Lava.

Who they are

Eric Moe is a Guggenheim fellow, honoured by the American Academy of Arts and Letters. His Laminar Flow in Upsidedown Creek opens the season in Currents.

Eli Tausen á Lava is nominated for the 2026 Nordic Council Music Prize.

Paul Novak and Sam Wu have both taken the ASCAP Foundation's Morton Gould Young Composer Award, Novak with the Leo Kaplan Award.

Zygmund de Somogyi, a Royal Philharmonic Society composer for 2025 and a Fromm Foundation fellow, is also Festival ADAR's composer in residence. Their score reached CreArtBox through the open call, and it closes the season in New York.

An evening of its own

On 23 April 2027, Pressure and Release is given over entirely to the call: seven works, seven composers, one evening at The DiMenna Center.

The open call has become the spine of the season. Eleven of the works we play this year came through it, more than in any season before, and in April an entire evening is given over to it.

The calls stay open. Composers can read the terms, and send a score, on the Opportunities page.
