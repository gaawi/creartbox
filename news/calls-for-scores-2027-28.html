Open call October 22, 2026

The Calls for Scores are open for the 2027/28 season

Two calls, one for composers anywhere and one for composers in the United States, open from 22 October to 15 December 2026. Any instrumentation, all career stages, and a first round read with no names attached.

CreArtBox is reading scores for the 2027/28 season. Two calls open today and close on 15 December 2026: CA1/2026 for composers of any nationality, and CA2/2026 for composers who are citizens or permanent residents of the United States. Both are read by the same jury, on the same dates, and both ask for two works.

Eleven of the works we are playing this season arrived through these calls. They are not a side programme: they sit in the same evenings as Brahms, Schumann, Stravinsky and Sibelius, and in April an entire night of the season is given over to them.

Send anything, for anything

Our core ensemble is flute, violin, viola, cello and piano, and those are the players who will read your score. But we accept works for any instrumentation. If a piece convinces us and the forces are not ours, we bring in the players it needs, or we talk to you about a version that works. That conversation happens after the selection and never without you.

Most of what we programme runs between 5 and 15 minutes and asks for no more than eight players. Treat that as guidance, not as a rule: a longer piece is read the same way, it simply has fewer places to go in a season. Works that have already been performed or recorded are welcome.

The first round is blind

Every score is prepared by someone outside the jury and arrives numbered, with the name, the biography, the website and the country taken out. Help us keep it clean: take your name off the score and out of the file name before you send it.

The second round is not blind, and that is deliberate. Once the music has been chosen on the music alone, we look at who wrote it, because by then the question is no longer whether a piece is good but which evening it belongs to and whether this is the start of something longer than one performance.

The jury is made up of members of the ensemble, who are the people who will have to play the piece, together with composers selected in earlier cycles of the call.

Dates

  • 22 October 2026, applications open.
  • 15 December 2026, applications close.
  • By spring 2027, every applicant hears back.
  • September 2027, the season is announced with the selected works in it.

Two scores in PDF, a recording or a MIDI realisation for each, the title, year, duration and instrumentation, and a short note on each piece. There is a $20 administrative fee, which pays for the selection and nothing else. Everything goes through the form; we do not read submissions sent by email.

It takes months, and we would rather be slow than skim. Send us something.
