From 5 to 9 October, Guillermo Laporta and Josefina Urraca spend five days at the Kuopio Conservatory in Finland: masterclasses with the students, and a workshop on building a chamber piece on stage, working closer to the audience and drawing on design and other disciplines.

One concert during those days is open to the public. It is on 6 October at 7:00 pm, in the Kamarimusiikkisali of the Kuopio Music Centre, next door to the conservatory. Ticketing is handled locally in Kuopio.

The rest of the week is work: the residency itself is closed, as teaching weeks are.

For masterclass or press enquiries, write to [info@creartbox.nyc](mailto:info@creartbox.nyc).
