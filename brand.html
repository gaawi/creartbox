Logo, colour, type

§ 01The logo.

The logo

Respect zoneClear space on every side equal to the height of the text box. Nothing enters it, including the edge of the page.
Minimum width120 px on screen, 30 mm in print. Below that, use the monogram.
Proportion7.18:1 for the horizontal wordmark. Scale it, never stretch it.
Over an imageUse the reversed logo on a dark or darkened area. The wedge keeps its amber.

Do

  • ✓

    Right. The reversed logo on a dark ground, with clear space around it.

  • ✓

    Right. The primary logo on a light ground, for print and pale surfaces.

  • ✓

    Right. Over a photograph, darken the area first. The logo sits on a scrim, not on the picture.

Don't

Each of these is the real logo file with one thing done to it.

  • ✗

    Stretched. The proportion is 7.18:1. Scale it; do not distort it.

  • ✗

    Squashed. The same error the other way. Set width or height, never both.

  • ✗

    Rotated. The logo is always level.

  • ✗

    Recoloured. Off-white and amber, or a single flat colour. Nothing else.

  • ✗

    Effects. No shadow, no glow, no outline, no gradient.

  • ✗

    Faded. To make it quieter, use a quieter ink - not transparency.

  • CreArtBox✗

    Retyped. Do not rebuild the wordmark in another typeface. Use the file.

  • ✗

    Too small. Below 120 px wide, or 30 mm in print, use the monogram instead.

  • Text crowding the mark, hard against the edge of the frame.
    ✗

    Respect zone breached. Keep clear space on every side equal to the height of the text box.

  • ✗

    On a busy picture. The mark is unreadable and the photograph is spoiled.

§ 02One signal per screen.

Colour

Four surfaces, five weights of off-white ink, and one amber. Use the amber once per screen, on the thing you want clicked or read first. Festival ADAR uses the same palette with blue in place of the amber.

Surfaces

  • --sala#070706Page ground
  • --foso#100F0ECards and raised surfaces
  • --filete#2B2825Rules and dividers
  • --filete-alto#3D3934Rules that need to be seen

Ink

  • --papel#F2EFE8Headings and primary text17.5:1 on --sala
  • --papel-medio#C9C3B9Body text11.5:1 on --sala
  • --papel-dim#9C968CSecondary text6.9:1 on --sala
  • --papel-tenue#8A847AThe lightest ink allowed on text5.4:1 on --sala
  • --papel-mudo#6B655DRules and icons only, never text3.5:1 on --sala

Signal

  • --senal#FFC403One signal per screen12.6:1 on --sala
  • --senal-adar#4FA3E3Festival ADAR, replacing the signal7.4:1 on --sala
  • --sobre-senal#0B0705Text set on the signal12.6:1 on --senal

Ratios are measured against the ground each colour is actually used on, to WCAG 2.1. --papel-mudo falls below 4.5:1 and is therefore restricted to rules and icons; it is never used for text.

§ 03Two typefaces.

Typography

Display

Literata

Used for titles, work titles and pull quotes. Work titles go in italic.

Titles: clamp(44px, 9vw, 104px) at a line height of 0.94.

Text

Archivo

Used for body copy, labels, dates and tables.

Body: 17px at 1.62. Labels: 11px, weight 600, letter-spacing 0.16em, uppercase. Figures set with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so columns of dates and durations line up.

Both are open-source and free to install. Fall back to Georgia and Helvetica respectively.

§ 04Widths and spacing.

Layout

Page width--ancho-max · 1120px maximum, whatever the window.
Measure--medida · 36em maximum for running text.
Section air--aire-secc · clamp(64px, 9vw, 120px) above and below each section.
CornersSquare. Nothing is rounded, including buttons.
Rules1px, in --filete.

Naming

reversed is light ink for dark grounds. primary and black are for light grounds. solid means one colour throughout, with no amber wedge. box means the mark is centred in a square, for icons.
