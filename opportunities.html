Frequently asked

FAQ.

Is there a submission fee?

No. All our open calls are free to enter, and they always will be. We received over 500 applications in our 2022 call alone — none of those composers paid.

Can I submit from outside the United States?

Yes. All calls are open internationally. CreArtBox has presented composers and visual artists from more than 20 countries.

Will the ensemble revise my piece?

We work closely with composers in rehearsal. Final revisions are always your decision.

Do you cover travel for the premiere?

For the Composer-in-Residence program: yes. For other calls: case by case.

Is there an age limit?

No. CreArtBox has premiered work by composers across a wide range of ages.

How long until I hear back?

Composer-in-Residence: quarterly review. Other calls: within 8 weeks of the deadline.
