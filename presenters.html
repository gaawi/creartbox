Programmes, repertoire and dates

Programmes on offer.

CreArtBox tours as a piano quintet: flute, piano, violin, viola and cello. Below are two programmes ready to take on the road. Both can be shortened or lengthened to fit a series, and we are glad to build a programme with a presenter from the repertoire the ensemble has already played.

On offerTwo programmes.
Program 1

Dvořák and the Schumanns

Warmth, lyricism, and rhythmic vitality bring together the music of Dvořák and the Schumanns. A flute arrangement of Dvořák's beloved "American" Quartet lends a fresh colour to its expansive melodies and spirited exchanges, before Robert Schumann's Piano Quartet draws us into a world of tenderness and Romantic intensity. After the intermission, Clara Schumann's Three Romances offer a moment of intimate reflection. Dvořák's Piano Quintet No. 2 brings the evening to an exuberant close, balancing songful expression with the irresistible energy of dance.

  • Antonín DvořákString Quartet No. 12 in F major, Op. 96, "American" (arrangement with flute) 24'
  • Robert SchumannPiano Quartet in E-flat major, Op. 47 25'
  • Intermission
  • Clara SchumannThree Romances, Op. 22 9'
  • Antonín DvořákPiano Quintet No. 2 in A major, Op. 81 40'

Music, about 98 minutes, with one intermission.

Program 2

Ravel, Kurtág, Ligeti, Stravinsky

Ravel's luminous sound world meets the bold imagination of Hungarian composers György Kurtág and György Ligeti in a first half shaped by contrast and discovery. The delicate fairy-tale scenes of Ma mère l'Oye sit alongside the concentrated gestures of Kurtág's Tre pezzi and the rhythmic wit of Ligeti's Musica ricercata, creating a dialogue between French colour and Hungarian invention. Ravel's Kaddish offers an expressive moment of contemplation before the intermission. Stravinsky's Petrushka then opens the door to a vivid carnival world, bringing the evening to a close with bustling dances, theatrical drama, and dazzling musical contrasts.

  • Maurice RavelMa mère l'Oye 16'
  • György KurtágTre pezzi 7'
  • György LigetiMusica ricercata 15'
  • Maurice RavelKaddish 5'
  • Intermission
  • Igor StravinskyPetrushka 40'

Music, about 83 minutes, with one intermission.

Both programmes can be adapted to suit different concert lengths. For a programme built around a theme, an anniversary or a local commission, write to info@creartbox.nyc: the ensemble has played everything listed here, and reads new scores every season through its open call.

The ensembleAbout CreArtBox.

CreArtBox is a New York City-based chamber music ensemble founded in 2013 by flutist Guillermo Laporta and pianist Josefina Urraca, together with core members Emilie-Anne Gendron (violin), Matthew Cohen (viola), and Julia Yang (cello). Now in its twelfth season, the ensemble performs regularly in New York City and tours nationally and internationally, with appearances across the United States, Spain, Europe, and Japan.

CreArtBox has earned recognition from major arts publications: The New Yorker named the ensemble among its art and music top picks, and BroadwayWorld described its performances as "wholly authentic, visually and aurally compelling." The ensemble's work has been supported by grants and awards from the New York State Council on the Arts, the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs, and the Amphion Foundation, among other funders.

Since its founding season, CreArtBox has run an open Call for Scores, through which it has premiered works by Pulitzer Prize-winning composers alongside emerging voices, and mentored composers early in their careers.

Guillermo Laporta (flute) is a Spanish flutist, composer, and designer, and Artistic Director of CreArtBox and Festival ADAR. Josefina Urraca (piano) is a Spanish pianist and student of Dmitri Bashkirov, whose playing exemplifies "introspection and musical abandon." Violinist Emilie-Anne Gendron has been lauded by The Strad for her "marvelous and lyrical playing" and appears with major orchestras and ensembles. Violist Matthew Cohen, a special prize winner at the Primrose International Viola Competition, is described as one of the most sought-after violists of his generation. Cellist Julia Yang, praised for "her sense of joyful virtuosity," is a founding member of the Naumburg-winning Merz Trio. Beyond its core chamber programs, CreArtBox also produces larger-scale productions and co-produces Festival ADAR, an international arts festival held in rural Asturias, Spain.
