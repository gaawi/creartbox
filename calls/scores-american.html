Call for Scores
American composers

Call for Scores ·
American composers.

Open now, until 15 December 2026. Scores received by the deadline are read for the 2027/28 season. Everyone who applies hears back within eight weeks of the deadline.

A parallel call dedicated to U.S.-based composers. Selected works are programmed on the New York Series at The DiMenna Center for Classical Music and on touring engagements.

What we're seeking

  • Original chamber compositions: duos, trios, or up to 8 musicians.
  • Duration between 5 and 15 minutes.

Eligibility

  • U.S. citizens or permanent residents.
  • No restrictions based on experience or age.

What to send

  • Two scores in PDF format.
  • A recording or a MIDI realisation for each, as a file or a link.
  • A short programme note and a short biography.
  • Everything goes through the application form; we do not read submissions sent by email.

Dates

  • 22 October 2026, applications open.
  • 15 December 2026, applications close.
  • By mid-February 2027, every applicant hears back.
  • September 2027, the season is announced with the selected works in it.
  • October 2027 to August 2028, the performances.

What selected composers receive

Selected works are performed in the 2027/28 season, rehearsed with the ensemble and recorded, and are programmed alongside the standard repertoire rather than set apart in a new-music evening. Eleven of the works played in the 2026/27 season arrived through the ensemble's calls. Works are integrated into interdisciplinary productions combining music with visual art and theatre, and past participants have received $10,000 each in commissions, with works taken on tour in the United States and Europe.
