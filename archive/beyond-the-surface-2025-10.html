Beyond the Surface

To live is to struggle with appearances. We move through our days on the thin surface of things, satisfied with what shines before our eyes and pleases our ears. Yet beneath every surface lies an abyss-mystery, longing, and the search for truth. Music, if we give ourselves to it, can lift that veil.
