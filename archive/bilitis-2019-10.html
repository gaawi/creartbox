Bilitis

CreArtBox and Parhelion Trio come together to inaugurate their 2019/20 concert series, drawing inspiration from Claude Debussy’s Songs of Bilitis. The program encompasses a variety of compositions, including Philip Glass's "Music in Similar Motion," Seong Ae Kim's new commission, Beethoven's "Sonata in D op.6," Debussy's "The Songs of Bilitis," and Camille Saint-Saëns's "Tarantelle, Op.6."

Performers

  • Parhelion Trio
  • Sarah Carrier, flute
  • Ashleé Miller, clarinet
  • Andrea Christie, piano
  • CreArtBox
  • Guillermo Laporta, flute
  • Josefina Urraca, piano
  • Nuno Marques, Piano and Celesta
  • Jacopo Rampini, Reciter
