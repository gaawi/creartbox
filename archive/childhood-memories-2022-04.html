Childhood Memories

Childhood memories are delicate treasures that reside deep within our hearts. They are fragments of innocence, painted with the vibrant colors of joy and wonder. These memories transport us to a time when the world was a magical place filled with endless possibilities.

Performers

  • Yi Qun Xu, cello
  • Josefina Urraca, piano
