Part of: Circle Music Series at the William Vale

Circle Noir (Part I)

Step into a realm where classical music takes center stage on a remarkable 360-degree platform, enveloping the audience in an immersive sonic and visual journey. Here, the musicians are at the heart of a circular stage, encircled by the audience, blurring the line between performer and observer. As the music unfolds, cutting-edge projection and sound design technologies converge to create an unparalleled immersive experience.
