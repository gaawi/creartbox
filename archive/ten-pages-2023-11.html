Ten pages of a yet to be written book open to new ideas and new horizons. CreArtBox celebrates its 10th anniversary of creating and producing live art performances, merging classical and contemporary music. In 2013 we presented 'Pictures at an Exhibition' by Modest Mussorgsky. As an homage to that concert, we will revisit it, combining it with new music composed by our resident composer Andrea Casarrubios, Saint-Saens, and Rachmaninov.
