Festival ADAR 2023

ADAR's third edition expanded to thirteen actions spanning four municipalities. Two long-running installations - the Capilla de los Dolores in Grado and the Auditorio As Quintas in El Franco - framed a week of visual concerts, sound walks, micro-concerts, and a closing performance with video mapping in Leiguarda.
