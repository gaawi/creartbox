Festival ADAR 2025

ADAR's fifth edition runs almost two weeks across rural Asturias, with 11 actions spanning chamber concerts, sound walks, micro-recitals in hórreos, and visual installations.

Highlights include the opening of The Whale with live music, narration and audiovisual creation at the Monasterio San Antolín de Bedón; a sound walk through the cloister of the Parador de Corias; and a closing recital featuring cellist Andrea Casarrubios, 2025 Grammy nominee, at the Iglesia de Leiguarda.
