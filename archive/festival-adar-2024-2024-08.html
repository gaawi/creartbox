Festival ADAR · IV edition

Festival ADAR 2024

The fourth edition opened with a four-day art installation in Grado and unfolded across monasteries, palaces, hórreos, and the heart of Leiguarda over ten days.

Highlights: a concert at the Monasterio de San Antolín de Bedón in Llanes, the visual concert in the Mercado de Pola in Pola de Somiedo, and the closing weekend in Belmonte de Miranda with the now-traditional sidra and empanada gathering.
