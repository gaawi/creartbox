Festival ADAR 2024

Asturias, Spain

Festival ADAR, held August 8–18 in Asturias, Spain, is a vibrant celebration of rural arts, spanning across villages like Llanes, Salas, and Pola de Somiedo. Dedicated to cultural revitalization, it transforms local heritage sites into immersive spaces for art, music, and visual installations. ADAR unites multidisciplinary artists in artist residencies and public performances, fostering a unique rural-urban cultural exchange. The festival is organized by the Asociación para el Desarrollo de las Artes en el Medio Rural, aiming to enrich community life and preserve regional heritage. For more details, visit Festival ADAR.
