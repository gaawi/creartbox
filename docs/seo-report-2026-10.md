One tool owns all of it, `tools/build_seo.py`, writing one block per
page between `<!-- SEO ... -->` markers. It has a `--check` mode like
every other generator in `tools/`, so the state can be verified rather
than remembered. `tools/seo_block.py` lets the five generators that
rewrite whole pages carry that block over instead of wiping it.

**Every fact in the markup is read from the page it describes.** The
only two exceptions live at the top of `build_seo.py` where they can be
audited: `VENUES`, which holds street addresses for the two venues
confirmed against the venue itself, and `TICKETS`, which holds prices
exactly as the seller showed them, with the date they were read.

## 3. AI search specifically

What an assistant needs is not a keyword: it is an unambiguous entity
and a set of facts it can quote without hedging.

- **One organization entity**, `https://creartbox.nyc/#organization`,
  declared on all 141 pages with the same `@id`: legal name, founding
  year and city, founders, the five members, 501(c)(3) status, the
  Queens address, the genres, the social profiles in `sameAs` and the
  DiMenna Center as its `location`. Every other node points at it.
- **Concerts are machine-readable.** Currents carries its start and end
  in the venue's own time zone, the place with its postal address, the
  five performers linked to their own `Person` pages, the programme as
  five `MusicComposition` nodes with composer and running time, and the
  offer: $15 to $70, USD, in stock, with the Eventbrite URL.
- **The archive is machine-readable too.** 80 past performances with
  their dates, venues and repertoire. This is what lets an assistant
  answer "has CreArtBox played Clara Schumann before".
- **`robots.txt` names the assistant crawlers as welcome** -- GPTBot,
  OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot,
  Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Bingbot, CCBot and others. This
  is a choice, not a default: it also permits model training. One line
  per agent reverses any of it.
- **Two FAQ sets are marked up as `FAQPage`**, on the new visitor guide
  and on the opportunities hub, which is the format assistants quote
  most readily.

## 4. Selling seats for Currents

The page opened with "Currents assembles five works around a single
idea". Good second sentence, poor first one: the city appeared only as
a street address, and the ticket button sat below nine paragraphs.

- Title and description now carry the composers, the city, the date and
  the starting price.
- A lead paragraph states who plays, where, when, how long it runs and
  what a seat costs. The essay follows untouched, and the home page
  still quotes the essay rather than the logistics.
- A ticket button sits directly under the date line, with "From $15 ·
  on sale now through Eventbrite" beside it. **On a 390px phone it is
  the first thing under the heading**, where it used to be nine
  screens down.
- "Get tickets" in the masthead of that page goes to the Eventbrite
  listing for that concert rather than to the calendar.
- The home page hero carries one line, above the fold on a phone:
  *Next concert — Currents · Friday, October 30, 2026 · 7:30 pm · The
  DiMenna Center, New York, NY.* It is generated by
  `sync_next_event.py`, so it follows the calendar.
- **`classical-concerts-nyc.html`** is a new page for the question the
  calendar does not answer: the hall, the subway lines, the start time,
  the running time, the price, and eight questions with answers. The
  season dates come from the calendar, so archiving a concert removes
  it from this page too.

- Every one of 1,912 images declares `alt`. The 61 archive thumbnails

  instead of repeating the title, which is also what its social card

- Nine pages shared four titles. All unique.
- **Two dead links fixed**: the Android app was listed as

- Every generator in `tools/` passes `--check`, including the five
  new or changed ones.

## 7. Measurement

No analytics is installed, and none was installed here: that is your
decision to make, not mine.

What exists is the wiring. Ticket and apply buttons carry
`data-cb-track` with the context beside it, and `initConversions()` in
`site.js` hands a click to `gtag`, Plausible or a `dataLayer` if one is
ever loaded. It does nothing while none is, and it never stands between
a visitor and the checkout.

**A click is not a sale.** What this can count is how many people leave
for Eventbrite or Tally. How many of them complete is a number only
Eventbrite and Tally hold, and the two should be compared, not
confused.

The highest-value thing left is free and takes ten minutes: verify
`creartbox.nyc` in **Google Search Console** and **Bing Webmaster
Tools**, submit the sitemap, and request indexing of the Currents page
and the two call pages. Bing matters more than its market share
suggests, because it feeds several assistants.

## 8. What I could not do, and what is still wrong

- **Nothing is pushed or deployed.** Six commits sit on local `main`.
- **No listing was submitted anywhere.** `docs/external-listings.md` is
  the prioritised list, with how far each submission path was actually
  confirmed.
- **The Spotify tile on `media.html` is broken.**
  `open.spotify.com/artist/creartbox` answers "page not available". It
  needs the real artist URL, which only you have, so the markup was
  left as it is rather than guessed at.
- **`play.creartbox.nyc` returns a 500** -- a PHP version error from
  the WordPress install behind it. 117 pages link to it. That is a
  server to fix, not a file in this repository.
- **Prices are verified for Currents only.** Every other concert's
  offer carries the link and availability and no price, because no
  price was published to read. Add them to `TICKETS` as the listings
  go on sale, with the date you read them.
- **Two concert pages have no city.** `chapel-restoration-2027` and
  `gettysburg-community-concert-2027` give a venue name and nothing
  else, so their markup carries a place with no address. A presenter or
  an audience cannot tell where either one is either.
- **Six archive pages have no venue** in their description line, so
  they no longer produce an event at all: `circle-noir-part-ii`,
  `echoes-of-solitude`, `masked-sounds`, `mountains-we-love-to-climb`,
  `sounds-of-the-dome`, `ten-pages`. Add the venue and the event
  returns on the next run.
- **Legacy newsletter markup inside `archive.html`** carries a Google
  Podcasts link to a service that closed and an Apple Podcasts URL that
  errors. It is historical content and was left alone apart from giving
  its images alt text.
- **`/classical-concerts-nyc` without the extension** depends on GitHub
  Pages serving `.html` implicitly. The canonical URL points at the
  `.html` form, which always works.
- **Two external sites could not be reached from here**,
  `chambermusiclouisville.org` and `matthewcohenviola.com`. They may be
  fine; the connection failed, which is not the same as a broken link.
