2023Mar24-26

Architecture of a Common Man

Part of: New Productions
Culture Lab Theater, New York City

Architecture of a Common Man is an opera-ballet-film performance that follows the journey of a world-recognized photographer working on a documentary about his own life, aiming to teach others how to be successful. While creating this documentary, he unveils a fascinating retrospective of his life, analyzing his relationship with work, family, health, and spirituality. Eventually discovering parts of himself that were buried in his professional ambitions.

2011-12Tour

Noctum

Part of: Touring Productions
Spanish Tour

"Noctum" is a multimedia opera set against the backdrop of one of humanity's first lunar eclipses. This production weaves together a chamber ensemble, physical actor, aerialist, and soprano to tell the haunting tale of Érebo, a mysterious creature yearning to experience the beauty of dreams. His misguided quest to steal the moon unleashes a cascade of nightmares, shattering the once-peaceful dream world. As Érebo's desperation grows, the goddess Elhüa races against time to mend the shattered moon and save humanity from the torment of perpetual nightmares. "Noctum" invites spectators on a mesmerizing journey through the delicate balance between light and darkness, set to a backdrop of haunting melodies and breathtaking performances.

2009-10Tour

London The Show

Part of: Touring Productions & Live Art Shows
Spanish Tour

"LONDON THE SHOW" is a spectacular musical theater production that immerses the audience in the vibrant heartbeat of London. With over 100 talented artists, this groundbreaking show seamlessly blends cinema and live theater, accompanied by a dynamic fusion of live jazz, drum & bass, Indian, and classical music. Contemporary dance, acrobatics, video mapping, and a compelling narrative weave together, creating a mesmerizing experience that captures the essence of London's frenetic pace and its diverse population. Hailed as "a shocking staging" by El Pais, this production celebrates the city's energy and the universal connections that bind its people from all corners of the world.
