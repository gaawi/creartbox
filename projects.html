2006 – 2026

Six works, twenty years.

Each CreArtBox production is built around the form we call a visual concert: live music staged in dialogue with projection, light, and design. Some are operas. Some are multimedia chamber works. They all come from the same studio.

Definition

What a visual concert is.

Visual art and theatrical design techniques are utilized to enhance the audience's listening experience of live classical and contemporary music while respecting the original musical composition.

These multimedia performances are often presented in medium-size venues that allow the audience to be fully immersed in the atmosphere of the production created by lights and projections.

CreArtBox works with visual artists, designers, dancers, and other creative minds to craft multi-layered performances centered around classical music — operating with a roster of world-class musicians from the Metropolitan Opera Orchestra, the New York Philharmonic, the BBC Orchestra, and the Chamber Music Society of Lincoln Center.

Entry · 01 / 06
Opera-ballet

Two Roads

2020 / 2024 rev.

Materializes the inner thoughts of Elle, scrutinizing the choices she made throughout her life — an apocryphal attempt to write a letter to a future self.

Elle's character is constructed by overlapping a conglomeration of performers fluctuating in time and space. The work is framed as an opera, but the set, the choreography, and the light and sound design are written into the libretto, following a parallel creative process.

AWAVE

A multimedia concert experience for flute, piano, video, and electronics — a new creation by Guillermo Laporta.

Entry · 03 / 06
Full-scale music and theatre production

Visuality

2014 – 2016

A narrative inspired by studies by Sigmund Freud and Carl Rogers — a psychoanalyst exploring the deepest chambers of his mind through music, poetry, and observation.

Written by Guillermo Laporta and Jacopo Rampini, choreographed by Marissa Maislen. Fuses music by Sebastian Currier, Joshua Penman, Debussy, Paul Moravec, Jacob TV, Joan Tower, Marcos Fernández, and Frederic Rzewski. Dr. Reyn's inner mind is reflected in a commutable set — cubes surround the scene, the musicians, and the audience, using video-mapping to bring the world to life.

Entry · 04 / 06
Opera

Noctum

2011

An early CreArtBox opera — a meditation on night, presence, and the music made when an audience cannot quite see.

Produced during the European years of the project, Noctum laid the groundwork for what would later become the visual concert form. The piece tours under the CreArtBox banner today as a revived chamber-theatre production.

Cre.Art Project

2006

The first work. The founding project that gave the organization its first name and shape.

Conceived in Europe by Guillermo Laporta and clarinetist and performance creator Tagore Gonzalez. The piece was the seed of the organization that would, seven years later, be re-established in New York as CreArtBox.

Associated project

The ADAMR festival.

In 2021, CreArtBox established The Association for the Arts Development in Rural Areas of Spain. Its inaugural project, the Leiguarda Music Festival, was sponsored by the Belmonte de Miranda City Council and Caja Rural, with cellist Beatriz Blanco, baritone Nathaniel Sullivan, pianist Josefina Urraca, flutist Guillermo Laporta, composer Marcos Fernández, and host Laude Martínez.

The festival received recognition from National and Regional Television in Spain, including Telecinco and Asturias Public Television.
