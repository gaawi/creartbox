Editorial · I

What this is.

CreArtBox creates and produces live art performances, fusing classical and contemporary music with original ideas encouraged by multidisciplinary interaction. Visual art and theatrical design techniques are utilized to enhance the audience's listening experience while respecting the original musical composition.

Our programs are designed to support professional artists, encourage the creation of new meaningful work, make world-class performances accessible to everyone regardless of background or economic circumstance, inspire and educate future generations, revitalize local communities, and nurture new ways of human expression.

The ensemble maintains a well-established concert series in New York City, tours nationally with recurring engagements on the West Coast, and performs internationally in countries such as Japan, Spain, and the United Kingdom.

CreArtBox also runs an open call for scores and a composer residency program, fostering the creation of new works and mentoring emerging voices — from Pulitzer Prize winners like Caroline Shaw and David Lang to contemporary creators such as Timo Andres, Anna Clyne, Joshua Penman, Nico Muhly, and many others.
