About the series

The New York Series.

Since 2012, CreArtBox has presented its New York Series at The DiMenna Center for Classical Music. The flagship program showcases contemporary chamber music through fully produced, original productions that integrate sound, visual art, and stage design — approximately six productions per year, with world premieres of newly commissioned works, reimagined classics, and collaborations with video artists, filmmakers, and designers.

Recognized by The New Yorker as an "art and music top pick" and by TimeOut as "an ensemble devoted to multidisciplinary events." The series sold out to a full 250-seat capacity for its 2025/26 season opening, Beyond the Surface.
