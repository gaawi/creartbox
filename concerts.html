About the series

The New York Series.

Since 2012, CreArtBox has presented its New York Series at The DiMenna Center for Classical Music. Approximately six productions a year, with world premieres of newly commissioned works, reimagined classics, and collaborations with video artists, filmmakers, and designers.

The series launched at DiMenna in 2012, expanded to multiple NYC venues between 2018–2023, and returned to The DiMenna Center as its permanent home in 2024 with a fixed core ensemble.
