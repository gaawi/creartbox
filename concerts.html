About the series

The CreArt Music Series.

The heart of our New York season. Three concerts per year at The DiMenna Center for Classical Music — a laboratory for commissions, visual concert work, and chamber repertoire performed by core ensemble members and invited collaborators from the Metropolitan Opera Orchestra, the New York Philharmonic, and the BBC Orchestra.

Past seasons have presented work by Saariaho, Caroline Shaw, Anna Thorvaldsdóttir, Sebastian Currier, Steve Reich, John Harbison, Nico Muhly, Mario Diaz de Leon, Timo Andres, and more than fifty others.
