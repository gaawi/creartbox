Dear friends,

We hope you had a lovely summer. Our thirteenth season at The DiMenna Center
opens on October 30, and we would love for you to be in the room.

Violinist Emilie-Anne Gendron, violist Matthew Cohen and cellist Julia Yang
join us for a programme that begins with the players walking on from offstage
and ends with the cellist retuning mid-movement to reach a note the
instrument does not have.


THE PROGRAMME

  Guillermo Laporta   Brown Leaves Moving                      4'
  Johannes Brahms     Trio in A minor, Op. 114 - I             8'
  Eric Moe            Laminar Flow in Upsidedown Creek         5'
