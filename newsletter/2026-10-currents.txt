Subject:   Currents opens the season, 30 October at The DiMenna Center
Preheader: Brahms, both Schumanns, Eric Moe and a new piece by Guillermo Laporta.


Dear friends,

We hope you're doing well and that you've had a lovely summer. As autumn
approaches, we're getting ready for a new season of our New York Series at
The DiMenna Center, beginning with Currents on October 30. We'd love for you
to join us.

For this opening concert, we'll be joined by violinist Emilie-Anne Gendron,
violist Matthew Cohen, and cellist Julia Yang. The program brings together
music by Brahms, Clara Schumann, Eric Moe, and Guillermo Laporta, closing
with Robert Schumann's Piano Quartet.
