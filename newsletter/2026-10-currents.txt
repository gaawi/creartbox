Subject:   Currents opens the season, 30 October at The DiMenna Center
Preheader: Brahms, both Schumanns, Eric Moe and a new piece by Guillermo Laporta.


Dear friends,

We hope you're doing well and that you've had a lovely summer. As autumn
approaches, we're getting ready for a new season of our New York Series at
The DiMenna Center, beginning with Currents on October 30. We'd love for you
to join us.

For this opening concert, we'll be joined by violinist Emilie-Anne Gendron,
violist Matthew Cohen, and cellist Julia Yang. The program brings together
music by Brahms, Clara Schumann, Eric Moe, and Guillermo Laporta, closing
with Robert Schumann's Piano Quartet.

Our New York Series at DiMenna continues across four evenings, bringing
together classical and contemporary chamber music with the visual ideas that
shape our performances.

  30 OCTOBER 2026 - CURRENTS
  Five works around one idea: movement carried from one place to another.
  Brahms, both Schumanns, Eric Moe, and a new piece by Guillermo Laporta.

  11 DECEMBER 2026 - WINTERLIGHT
  The world premiere of Hannah Selin's Tectonic Lullaby for piano quintet,
  Ravel's Ma Mere l'Oye, and Stravinsky's Petroushka in a chamber arrangement.

  23 APRIL 2027 - PRESSURE AND RELEASE
  Seven works by seven composers, the whole evening drawn from our open Call
  for Scores.

  7 MAY 2027 - FEVERDREAM
  The season closes with Sibelius's G minor Piano Quintet, alongside new work
  by Zygmund de Somogyi and Eli Tausen a Lava.
