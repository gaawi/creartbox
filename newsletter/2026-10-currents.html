Dear friends,

We hope you had a lovely summer. Our thirteenth season at The DiMenna Center opens on October 30, and we would love for you to be in the room.

Violinist Emilie-Anne Gendron, violist Matthew Cohen and cellist Julia Yang join us for a programme that begins with the players walking on from offstage and ends with the cellist retuning mid-movement to reach a note the instrument does not have.
