Dear friends,

We hope you’re doing well and that you’ve had a lovely summer. As autumn approaches, we’re getting ready for a new season of our New York Series at The DiMenna Center, beginning with Currents on October 30. We’d love for you to join us.

For this opening concert, we’ll be joined by violinist Emilie-Anne Gendron, violist Matthew Cohen, and cellist Julia Yang. The program brings together music by Brahms, Clara Schumann, Eric Moe, and Guillermo Laporta, closing with Robert Schumann’s Piano Quartet.

The season ahead

Our New York Series at DiMenna continues across four evenings, bringing together classical and contemporary chamber music with the visual ideas that shape our performances.

30 October 2026

Currents

Five works around one idea: movement carried from one place to another. Brahms, both Schumanns, Eric Moe, and a new piece by Guillermo Laporta.

11 December 2026

Winterlight

The world premiere of Hannah Selin’s Tectonic Lullaby for piano quintet, Ravel’s Ma Mère l’Oye, and Stravinsky’s Petroushka in a chamber arrangement.

23 April 2027

Pressure and Release

Seven works by seven composers, the whole evening drawn from our open Call for Scores.

7 May 2027

Feverdream

The season closes with Sibelius’s G minor Piano Quintet, alongside new work by Zygmund de Somogyi and Eli Tausen á Lava.

We have also rebuilt creartbox.nyc. Every concert now has its own page with the full programme and the players, the printed programme book can be read online before you come, and there is a biography for each member of the ensemble.

 

We spent August in Asturias, Spain, working on the sixth edition of Festival ADAR. Bringing these projects to life is a different side of our work, and we’re happy to share a little of it with our New York community. Take a look at this summer’s festival →

Next stop is Kuopio, Finland, where we’ll give a concert at the Kuopio Music Centre on October 6, alongside a residency at Kuopio Conservatory working with students. Then it’s back to New York — and hopefully to seeing many of you on October 30.

Warmly,

Josefina & Guillermo
