October 28, 2025 · Season opening

Beyond the Surface — CreArtBox opens its 2025/26 season

CreArtBox opens its 2025/26 New York Series with Beyond the Surface at The DiMenna Center for Classical Music, reaching its full 250-seat capacity. A reflection on what lies under the visible — programmed alongside new commissions and visual collaborations.

April 2025 · Now on YouTube

Caroline Shaw · Thousandth Orange

A new performance of Caroline Shaw's Thousandth Orange, filmed in an immersive environment combining sculpture and light installations. The composition explores shifting fragments — where color, repetition, and musical line create a cohesive artistic experience.

Watch on YouTube → play.creartbox.nyc
September 6, 2025 · Festival ADAR & Season Preview

Another edition of art and music in rural Asturias

Festival ADAR returns to Belmonte with strengthened artist residencies in the village of Leiguarda and enhanced visual-musical programming. The inaugural concert took place at the Monastery of San Antolín de Bedón. A preview of the upcoming CreArtBox season anchors the announcement.

festivaladar.com →

Approaching Zero at Flushing Town Hall

A free CreArtBox concert at Flushing Town Hall — RSVP only. Part of our community engagement programming in Queens, in partnership with FTH and the Queens Council on the Arts.
