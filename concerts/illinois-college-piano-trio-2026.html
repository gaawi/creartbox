Piano Trio · Illinois College

November 6, 2026 · Rammelkamp Chapel · Illinois College campus, Jacksonville, IL

A piano-trio programme that frames Shostakovich's astonishing teenage Trio in C minor between the Boulanger sisters - Nadia's stark Modéré and Lili's late Nocturne - and closes with Rachmaninoff's first elegiac trio. Bloch, Casarrubios, and Kurtág complete a programme about grief, dedication, and the inheritance of musical voice.

Program

  • Nadia BoulangerThree Pieces for cello and piano - No. 1, Modéré 3'
  • Dmitri ShostakovichPiano Trio No. 1 in C minor, Op. 8 14'
  • Lili BoulangerNocturne for violin and piano 4'
  • Ernest BlochThree Nocturnes for Piano Trio 11'
  • Andrea CasarrubiosSilbo · and · 24 Mozas 10'
  • György KurtágTre Pezzi 13'
  • Sergei RachmaninoffTrio élégiaque in G minor, No. 1 15'

Performers

About CreArtBox

CreArtBox is a New York–based chamber music and multimedia ensemble founded in 2013, performing at The DiMenna Center for Classical Music and on tour internationally. The ensemble's New York Series presents approximately six productions a year - combining commissioned premieres, reimagined classics, and collaborations with visual artists, filmmakers, and writers.

In parallel, CreArtBox runs Festival ADAR each August in rural Asturias, Spain - an international arts festival pairing chamber music with site-specific performance in churches, hórreos, and mountain villages. Both the New York Series and Festival ADAR are produced by CreArtBox, Inc., a 501(c)(3) non-profit.
