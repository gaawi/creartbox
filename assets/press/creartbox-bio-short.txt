CreArtBox — biography (short, ≈80 words)

CreArtBox is a New York-based chamber music and multimedia ensemble
led by flutist Guillermo Laporta and pianist Josefina Urraca, with core
members Emilie-Anne Gendron, Matthew Cohen, and Julia Yang. Recognized
by The New Yorker as one of its "art and music top picks," praised by
Time Out as "an ensemble devoted to multidisciplinary events," and
described by BroadwayWorld as "wholly authentic, visually and aurally
compelling," the ensemble creates immersive performances that merge
classical and contemporary music with original stage design and visual
art.
