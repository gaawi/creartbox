CreArtBox - biography (medium, ≈160 words)

CreArtBox is a New York-based chamber music and multimedia ensemble led
by flutist Guillermo Laporta and pianist Josefina Urraca, with core
members Emilie-Anne Gendron, Matthew Cohen, and Julia Yang. Recognized
by The New Yorker as one of its "art and music top picks," praised by
Time Out as "an ensemble devoted to multidisciplinary events," and
described by BroadwayWorld as "wholly authentic, visually and aurally
compelling," CreArtBox is celebrated for performances that merge
classical and contemporary music with original stage design and visual
art.

The ensemble's work has been supported by the National Endowment for
the Arts, the New York State Council on the Arts, the Queens Council on
the Arts, the Amphion Foundation, the Alice M. Ditson Fund, and the
Aaron Copland Fund for Music. It also runs an open call for scores and
a composer residency program, and co-produces Festival ADAR in
Asturias, Spain - dedicated to revitalizing rural communities through
music, residencies, and site-specific projects.
