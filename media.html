Section VI

The live podcast.

Since 2022, CreArtBox runs a live-podcast series with the Queens Public Library — a hybrid talk-show + chamber concert, free and open to the public. Composers, visual artists, and members of the ensemble speak about a single work, then perform it live.

Subscribe wherever you listen to podcasts, or attend a taping at the library.
