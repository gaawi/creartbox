Founded in New York City in 2013, CreArtBox is a chamber music ensemble that thinks like a theatre company — and a non-profit that runs a magazine, a podcast, a streaming archive, a residency, and two festivals on two continents.

§ 01 · The biography

An ensemble, and an idea.

CreArtBox creates and produces live art performances, fusing classical and contemporary music with original ideas encouraged by multidisciplinary interaction.

Our programs are designed to support professional artists, encourage the creation of new meaningful work, make world-class performances accessible to everyone regardless of background or economic circumstance, inspire and educate future generations, revitalize local communities, and nurture new ways of human expression.

The organization was founded in New York City in 2013 by pianist Josefina Urraca and flutist Guillermo Laporta, who continue to serve as the organization's artistic directors. The project was originally conceived in 2006 in Europe as “Cre.Art Project” by Guillermo Laporta and clarinetist and performance creator Tagore Gonzalez.

With its headquarters in New York City and Asturias, Spain, CreArtBox develops an array of distinctive platforms to pursue its mission, including a music festival in Queens, a printed magazine, a classical music streaming platform, a live music podcast, international touring performances, a residency and commissioning program for composers, and a festival to develop the arts in rural Spain.

CreArtBox works with visual artists, designers, dancers, and other creative minds to craft multi-layered performances centered around classical music, operating with a roster of world-class classical musicians, including members of orchestras such as the Metropolitan Opera Orchestra, the New York Philharmonic, the BBC Orchestra, and the Chamber Music Society of Lincoln Center.

Grants and awards from public and private institutions sustain CreArtBox's nonprofit mission alongside a group of generous individual supporters, maintaining steady yearly growth.

§ 05Selected milestones

A history, in entries.

A selection from the organization's records. The complete history runs to many pages.

Nov 2022

NYSCA Grant

CreArtBox receives the NYSCA (New York State Council on the Arts) Support for Organizations Grant.

Sep 2022

CreArt Music Festival · Winter

Three nights at the Blue Gallery in Manhattan. A trilogy called Fragile Form. With Granados, Saint-Saëns, Caroline Shaw, Annamaria Kowalsky, Nikolayeva, Nico Muhly, Hannah Selin, Michel van der Aa, Mozart, Brian Wysocki, Ravel, Shostakovich and Nadia Boulanger.

Aug 2022

ADAMR Festival · 2nd edition

CreArtBox presents the second edition of its eight-night festival in Spain for the development of rural areas.

May 2022

Queens Public Library partnership

CreArtBox launches a live-podcast music series with the Queens Public Library. Hybrid talk-show + chamber concert.

Mar 2022

500 applications

CreArtBox announces the winners of its Call for Submissions for contemporary music. Over 500 applications received.

Aug 2021

Founded in Spain

CreArtBox establishes The Association for the Arts Development in Rural Areas of Spain. Inaugural project: the Leiguarda Music Festival, with cellist Beatriz Blanco, baritone Nathaniel Sullivan, pianist Josefina Urraca, flutist Guillermo Laporta, composer Marcos Fernández, and host Laude Martínez.

Feb 2020

Two Roads · Tank Theater

Second production of the opera-ballet Two Roads. With dancers Mary Taylor Hennings and Can Wang, soprano Ariadne Greif, baritone Nathaniel Sullivan, actress Julie Berndt.

Dec 2019

AWAVE · Ryogoku Concert Hall

CreArtBox presents AWAVE at the Ryogoku Concert Hall in Japan. A multimedia concert experience for flute, piano, video, and electronics.

2013

CreArtBox founded · New York

Pianist Josefina Urraca and flutist Guillermo Laporta found CreArtBox in New York City. The project carries forward the work begun in 2006 as Cre.Art Project in Europe.

2006

Cre.Art Project

Original incarnation founded by Guillermo Laporta and clarinetist Tagore Gonzalez.
